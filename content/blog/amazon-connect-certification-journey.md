Back in mid-2000s, in São Paulo, I landed my first job as a call-center agent for a job-listing website. Throughout the next decade, I worked in many different positions (agent, SME, queue admin, supervisor), what gave me a comprehensive understanding of how customer service should be provided. Even though I changed paths at some point, I always brought my people skills acquired from those days to other tables I worked.

[tecRacer](https://www.tecracer.com/en/home-pt/) allowed me recently to be in touch with the rich and complex CX universe by introducing me to one of the hottest services AWS provides: [**Amazon Connect**](https://aws.amazon.com/connect/). Learning about this service made me reconnect (inevitable pun, sorry) to this almost-forgotten universe I was part of, allowing me to have a easier deep-dive journey.

I embarked on this intensive three-month journey by travelling from the industrial landscapes of Hannover to the vibrant streets of Barcelona, all in pursuit of mastering Amazon Connect. Today, I'm proud to announce that [I've successfully earned my **Amazon Connect Developer certification**](https://www.credly.com/earner/earned/badge/ed2aa08a-6f3b-4f99-a346-298229dfbc54) - and more importantly, tecRacer now has its first certified Amazon Connect professional in the Iberian Peninsula.

### The Certification Challenge

The Amazon Connect Developer certification exam is comprehensive and demanding. It covers:

- **Architecture Design**: Designing scalable and resilient Connect solutions
- **Development Skills**: Building custom integrations and Lambda functions
- **Security Implementation**: Ensuring compliance and data protection
- **Troubleshooting**: Identifying and resolving common issues
- **Best Practices**: Following AWS recommended patterns and practices

What made this certification particularly challenging was its practical focus. It's not enough to understand the theory - you need to demonstrate real-world problem-solving abilities and architectural thinking.

### Study Resources and Preparation

My preparation involved multiple learning channels:

1. **Official AWS Documentation**: The AWS Skill Builder has [a specific learning and badge plan](https://skillbuilder.aws/learning-plan/6YW2QYUBKS/amazon-connect-developer-learning--badge-plan/NEWQZB4HAQ) for the Developer certification
2. **Hands-on Labs**: Nothing beats practical experience
3. **Workshop Materials**: Detailed notes from Hannover and Barcelona sessions
4. **Practice Exams**: Testing knowledge and identifying gaps
5. **Real Project Work**: The tecRacer POC provided invaluable practical experience

### The beginning: tecRacer POC

Theory and workshops are one thing, but nothing beats real-world application. During my certification journey, I had the opportunity to contribute to a Proof of Concept (POC) project at tecRacer that perfectly showcased the power of Amazon Connect integration.

My part involved creating a sophisticated automation workflow:

1. **S3 Trigger Architecture**: When files arrive in designated S3 buckets, Lambda functions are automatically triggered
2. **Dynamic Task Creation**: These Lambda functions analyze the incoming files and create appropriate tasks in Amazon Connect flows
3. **Intelligent Routing**: Tasks are automatically routed to the right agents based on content analysis and business rules
4. **Real-time Processing**: The entire workflow operates in real-time, ensuring immediate response to customer needs

This hands-on experience was crucial for understanding how Amazon Connect integrates with the broader AWS ecosystem.

### Hannover Immersion

After the POC, I had an immersive Amazon Connect onboarding in Hannover delivered by senior tecRacer colleagues. Our German team have been involved in many Connect projects, so I was standing by the shoulder of giants, pretty much.

We covered:
- **Connect fundamentals**: A Q&A session to clarify everything related to Amazon Connect, and how to apply the AWS knowledge into the product
- **Contact Flow Design**: Understanding the visual flow builder and creating sophisticated routing logic
- **Lambda Integration**: Building serverless functions that seamlessly integrate with Connect workflows
- **API Management**: Leveraging the Connect API for custom integrations and automation
- **Customer Experience Optimization**: Best practices for creating exceptional customer journeys

### Barcelona: Hands-on Learning and Networking

The second phase took me to Barcelona, where theory met practice in the most engaging way possible. The Barcelona workshop, provided by AWS, focused on advanced implementation scenarios and real-world problem-solving.

The networking opportunities in Barcelona were invaluable. Connecting with other developers, architects, and AWS specialists provided insights that you simply can't get from documentation or online courses.

I attended two sessions: **Amazon Q in Connect** (learned how to apply AI functionalities to improve customer support) and **Contact Lens** (operation analytics to improve QA). I'll share further details on a tecRacer blog post - stay tuned!
